The National Human Rights Society (HAKAM; Malay: Persatuan Kebangsaan Hak Asasi Manusia) is a human rights non-governmental organisation (NGO) that works on human rights issues in Malaysia. HAKAM is considered to be one of the most vocal human rights NGOs in Malaysia. Anyone who is at least 18 years of age and a Malaysia citizen can be a member. There is a new member and renewal membership form.
